The Story

Some paths are not on any map.

At 14, I learned to survive the streets. At 22, I learned to survive prison. In 2009, I posted bond in Miami, walked out of jail, and made a decision that would erase my name for the next twelve years. I became a ghost - living under another name, working off the books in Corpus Christi tire shops, sleeping in shelters, carrying my laptop to the public library to stay alive. The broken chains on this road? I walked out of them. And then I carried them with me for a decade.

The Author

Miguel Rodriguez

I spent twelve years as a fugitive living under another name after walking away from charges in 2009. Before that, six years in prison. Since childhood, addiction. Today I live in Playa del Carmen, Quintana Roo, Mexico - sober, present, and finally using my real name. UNSEEN PATHS is not a how-to-run manual. It's what running does to your mind, your body, your fatherhood, and your soul.
